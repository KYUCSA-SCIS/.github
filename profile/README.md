# Kyambogo University Computing Students' Association (KYUCSA)

Official GitHub organization of the **Kyambogo University Computing Students' Association (KYUCSA)** at **Kyambogo University**, Kampala, Uganda.

## About KYUCSA

KYUCSA is the student-led computing community at Kyambogo University dedicated to helping students grow their technical skills, collaborate on practical projects, and build confidence in technology. We bring together students across different academic programmes and experience stages—from learners writing their first lines of code to peers building full applications and mentoring others.

## Core Focus Areas

- **Hands-On Learning & Workshops:** Practical sessions covering programming fundamentals, software engineering workflows, Git and GitHub, and modern development tools.
- **Collaborative Projects & Hackathons:** Team build sprints, problem-solving challenges, and open-source contributions that turn theory into working software.
- **Peer Mentorship & Onboarding:** Structured guidance that pairs newcomers with experienced students so beginners are supported rather than left behind.
- **Inclusive Leadership & Participation:** Creating welcoming spaces where students of all backgrounds, genders, financial situations, and accessibility needs can participate and step into leadership roles.

## Community Standards & Conduct

All members, contributors, mentors, speakers, event participants, and organisers are expected to uphold a respectful, inclusive, and safe environment across our events and online spaces.

- **Code of Conduct:** Please read our [Code of Conduct](../page/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for clear expectations on respectful behaviour, unacceptable conduct, incident reporting, and enforcement.
- **Respect & Accessibility:** We aim to design activities thoughtfully, communicate clearly, and foster a culture where questions and mistakes are treated as normal parts of learning.

## Contributing & Getting Involved

- **Explore Repositories:** Browse our organization repositories for training materials, open-source initiatives, and collaborative student projects.
- **Start With the Basics:** If you are new to Git and GitHub, check our beginner guides and workshop resources or reach out during community sessions.
- **Lead & Collaborate:** Share ideas, help review work, mentor peers, or contribute to upcoming community initiatives.
