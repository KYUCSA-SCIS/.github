# Kyambogo Student Developer Community — Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

Our student developer community exists to help students at Kyambogo University learn, build, collaborate, share knowledge, and explore opportunities in technology.

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and safe environment for everyone, regardless of gender, disability, age, academic programme, level of technical experience, financial background, nationality, or other personal characteristics.

This Code of Conduct applies to all community activities, including workshops, coding sessions, hackathons, project collaborations, online discussions, social events, and communication through community platforms.

It applies equally to community members, organisers, speakers, mentors, volunteers, and guests.

## 2. Expected Behaviour

All participants are expected to:

- Treat other members with respect, patience, and consideration.
- Welcome beginners and encourage people to ask questions without fear of embarrassment.
- Give constructive feedback that focuses on ideas and work rather than attacking individuals.
- Respect different opinions, experiences, backgrounds, and abilities.
- Give team members fair opportunities to contribute, learn, and lead.
- Credit people for their ideas, contributions, and work.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and consent.
- Ask for permission before taking or sharing identifiable photographs, recordings, or personal information.
- Use inclusive and respectful language.
- Help maintain an environment where people feel comfortable participating.
- Follow reasonable instructions from event organisers concerning safety and accessibility.
- Report behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct.

We encourage members to support one another, share knowledge, collaborate openly, and recognise that everyone is learning at a different pace.

## 3. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, sexual harassment, unwanted advances, or inappropriate sexual comments.
- Discrimination based on gender, disability, background, nationality, or other personal characteristics.
- Bullying, intimidation, threats, or deliberate humiliation.
- Insulting or mocking someone because they are a beginner or have limited technical knowledge.
- Deliberately excluding members from activities or opportunities because of who they are.
- Repeatedly interrupting, dismissing, or undermining another participant.
- Unwanted physical contact or behaviour that makes another person feel unsafe.
- Sharing private information, photographs, recordings, or conversations without permission.
- Taking credit for another person's work or deliberately misrepresenting contributions.
- Retaliating against someone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.
- Disrupting community activities through threatening, abusive, or discriminatory behaviour.

Disagreement about technical ideas is acceptable. Personal attacks and harassment are not.

## 4. Reporting an Incident

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, you are encouraged to report it.

You can report an incident to a community organiser or designated Code of Conduct contact. Reports may be made privately, in person, or through the community's designated reporting channel.

**Reporting contact:** [Insert the official community reporting email address]

When reporting an incident, you may provide:

- What happened.
- When and where it happened.
- The people involved, if known.
- Whether anyone may be at immediate risk.
- Any other information you feel comfortable sharing.

You do not need to investigate an incident yourself or confront the person involved before making a report.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Information will only be shared with people who need it to respond appropriately. We will not promise absolute confidentiality where sharing information is necessary to protect someone or meet university or legal obligations.

We will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

If someone is in immediate physical danger, contact university security or the appropriate emergency services rather than relying only on the community reporting process.

## 5. How We Respond to Violations

When a report is received, the designated organisers will:

1. Acknowledge the report where contact details are available.
2. Consider whether anyone is at immediate risk and take appropriate steps to protect their safety.
3. Listen to the people involved and gather relevant information fairly.
4. Handle the matter respectfully and avoid unnecessary sharing of private information.
5. Decide on an appropriate response based on the seriousness and circumstances of the incident.
6. Communicate relevant next steps to the people involved where appropriate.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

- A private reminder or warning.
- Asking someone to stop the behaviour immediately.
- Removing someone from an activity or online discussion.
- Temporarily suspending participation in community activities.
- Restricting access to community communication platforms.
- Removing someone from the community for serious or repeated violations.
- Referring serious matters to relevant university authorities or appropriate external authorities.

The community will take serious incidents seriously. A person's position, experience, popularity, or contribution to the community does not exempt them from this Code of Conduct.

## 6. Accessibility and Inclusion

We will make reasonable efforts to ensure that community activities are accessible and welcoming.

Organisers will consider venue accessibility, learning materials, communication methods, and the different needs of participants when planning activities.

Members are encouraged to communicate accessibility requirements privately to organisers so that reasonable arrangements can be considered.

We recognise that our resources may be limited, but we will be honest about our limitations and continue looking for ways to improve participation.

## 7. Scope and Application

This Code of Conduct applies during:

- Community workshops, training sessions, and meetups.
- Hackathons, coding challenges, and collaborative build sessions.
- Project meetings and mentorship activities.
- Community events organised in partnership with other groups.
- Online community platforms and group conversations.
- Other activities carried out in the name of the community.

Organisers and community leaders are responsible for helping enforce this Code of Conduct. All members share responsibility for maintaining a respectful environment.

## 8. Updates to This Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct will be reviewed periodically as the community grows and members' needs change.

Members may suggest improvements or raise concerns about how the Code is applied. Updates should be communicated clearly so that everyone understands the standards expected of them.

**Our principle:** Everyone deserves the opportunity to learn, contribute, and participate in technology without being harassed, discriminated against, or made to feel unwelcome.
